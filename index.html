Open tools for naval architecture

Web apps and open data for early-stage ship design, made for teaching and research. Each tool is a single web page: open it in a browser, nothing to install.

Tools and data

In active development in 2026. For education and early concept work: the results are estimates, not for design approval.

Ship Design Building Block Editor

2026  /  Design Building Block approach  /  DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23651032

Build a ship from 3D blocks for its spaces, grouped by function and linked to requirements. The tables sum volume, area, weight and centres of gravity.

An illustrative hull floats at the summed weight for a first check of draught, trim, heel and stability, with resistance, seakeeping and what-if variants.

Hull Generator

2026  /  DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23820204

Start from one of eleven hull types and a few main dimensions, then reshape the curves in three linked 2D views beside a live 3D hull.

Hydrostatics and a Holtrop resistance estimate update as you edit. The table of offsets loads straight into the Ship Design Building Block Editor.

Open the generator Code

GA to DBB

2026  /  Writes Ship Design Building Block Editor files  /  DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23887058

Reads a general arrangement drawing (vector PDF, DXF, SVG or a scanned picture) and turns its compartments into blocks, with the decks, a hull guess and the main particulars.

The app proposes and you check and correct, then save a project for the Ship Design Building Block Editor. The drawing never leaves your computer.

Open the app Code

Ship Evacuation

2026  /  Reads Ship Design Building Block Editor files  /  DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23816748

Persons from the IMO MSC.1/Circ.1533 population walk to the muster stations of a general arrangement, moved by a Social Force model and shown in 3D.

Gives the assembly time, arrival curve, door and stair flows and congestion. Fire and flooding can block routes.

Legacy

Earlier work, no longer maintained. Kept online as a reference for research and teaching; some demos may not run in current browsers.

  • Vessel.js

    2017–2025

    Open-source JavaScript library for conceptual ship design, with the vessel as an object: hydrostatics, stability, motions, marine operations, manoeuvring, fuel and energy.

    ExamplesCode

  • Digital twins of model ships

    2020–2026

    Two standards-based digital twins: live motion monitoring of a toy boat, and monitoring and control of a scale-model PSV in a wave basin.

    Basin replayDocsCode

  • Vessel.js with OpenBridge

    2021–2023

    Vessel.js combined with the OpenBridge bridge design system (AHO), with terrain and AIS traffic around Trondheim and Ålesund.

    TrondheimÅlesundCode

Early experimental web apps, 2013–2019

Small single-purpose apps from the first years of the lab, many made by master students. Most still run in the browser.

  • Parametric ship design

    2013

    A simple parametric design of an anchor handling tug supply vessel: from mission requirements to capabilities, sizing, criteria checks and a summary of the design.

    The first of the web apps

    Henrique M. Gaspar, with Ulstein International.

  • Lifecycle model for LNG-fuelled ships in the Arctic

    2014

    Epoch-era analysis of LNG-fuelled and conventional ships on Arctic routes under changing ice, rules and markets, comparing designs on CAPEX, OPEX and emissions. Presented at OMAE 2014.

    Henrique M. Gaspar, with Ulstein International.

  • Data-Driven Documents in conceptual ship design

    2014

    Interactive web visualisations of conceptual ship design data with the D3.js library, to help designers and stakeholders find knowledge early in the design. Presented at COMPIT 2014.

    Henrique M. Gaspar, Per Olaf Brett, Ali Ebrahim and Andre Keane.

  • Ship Design Layout Tool

    2015

    Proof of concept for layouts in conceptual design: blocks read from a CSV file are placed on a grid, with their neighbours and connections shown in a connection wheel.

    Henrique M. Gaspar, with the naval architecture group at UCL.

  • Ship Motion Application

    2015, updated 2024

    Heave, pitch, roll, vertical acceleration and wave bending moment from the main dimensions, with the closed-form expressions of Jensen, Mansour and Olsen (2004).

    Sthefano L. Andrade and Henrique M. Gaspar. Updated by Gaël Porée and Maxime Albouy (SeaTech, Toulon).

  • Roll and heave with anti-heeling tanks

    2015

    Animated linear roll and heave of a barge-like vessel in still water, with a moving-liquid tank.

    Thiago G. Monteiro, Jiafeng Xu and Henrique M. Gaspar.

  • Coupled tanks

    2016

    Two tanks joined by a pipe, with an optional pump: water levels and flow rate over time.

    Thiago G. Monteiro, Synnøve Nesdal Sandnes and Henrique M. Gaspar.

  • Hull from a table of offsets

    2016

    Lines plan and 3D hull plotted from a table of offsets in CSV, with a patrol craft and a fishing boat as examples.

    Jefferson Flor and Henrique M. Gaspar.

  • Holtrop resistance

    2016

    Resistance of a displacement ship by the Holtrop method, split into frictional, form, appendage, wave, bulb, transom and correlation parts.

    Jefferson Flor, Thiago G. Monteiro and Henrique M. Gaspar.

  • Hull Lines Generator

    2016

    Parametric hull lines from the main dimensions, inspired by X-bow offshore vessels. Curves that fail validation turn red.

    Thiago G. Monteiro and Henrique M. Gaspar.

  • Structural analysis

    2016

    Load distribution, shear force and bending moments to DNV rules, and sizing of longitudinal stiffeners.

    Sthefano L. Andrade.

  • Ship Motion Simulator

    2016

    Real-time 3D simulator of a ship virtual prototype: choose the bow, accommodation, propulsion and crane operation, then set the waves and motions.

    DNV GL COMPIT Award 2016

    Olivia Chaves, Elias Hasle and Henrique M. Gaspar.

  • Hull Scantlings Design Assistant

    2018

    Hull girder loads to DNV GL rules and a hull girder strength check, from the operating profile, main dimensions and cross-sections. Presented at ICSOS 2018.

    Olivia Chaves (master thesis), supervised by Henrique M. Gaspar and Henning Borgen (SINTEF Ålesund).

  • DBB Method parametric app

    2019

    Design Building Block app with a parametric hull, bulkheads and function blocks: hydrostatics and stability, Holtrop resistance and seakeeping. UCL and NTNU.

    Diogo Kramel, building on work by Alexander S. Piperakis and Henrique M. Gaspar (2016).

About the lab

The Ship Design and Operation Lab at NTNU studies how ships are designed and how they behave in maritime operations. We build virtual ship models, from the first sketch of the spaces to the ship at sea, and simulate their performance in the web browser.

The work is open by default: the tools and data on this page are free to use for teaching and research, and the code is on GitHub. The lab works closely with industry through the Blue Maritime Cluster.

Research areas

  • Fast-track and data-driven ship design
  • 3D models through the design process
  • Virtual prototyping and simulation in the browser
  • Product lifecycle management and modularisation

Disclaimer

The tools are for education and early concept work. Hulls are illustrative, and weights, hydrostatics, stability, resistance and evacuation times are simplified estimates. Do not use the results for design approval, class or flag-state documentation, or any safety-critical decision.
